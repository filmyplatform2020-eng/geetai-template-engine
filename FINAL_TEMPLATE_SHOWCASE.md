## Final Recommendations

### 1. Default Theme → **Cosmic Purple**
- Highest overall score (8.6)
- Established brand identity — already the default
- Most versatile across product categories
- Premium glassmorphism reads as "premium tech"
- Aurora background adds depth without performance cost

### 2. Best Premium Theme → **Gold Noir**
- Highest luxury feel score (10/10)
- Minimal visual noise — content stays primary
- Solid background = best performance
- Perfect for luxury watches, jewellery, premium fashion

### 3. Best Tech Theme → **Tech Blue**
- Highest trust score alongside Emerald (9/10)
- Clean gradient background — professional
- Familiar blue accent signals reliability
- Best for enterprise SaaS, business laptops, B2B

### 4. Best Minimal Theme → **Obsidian**
- Highest readability score (9/10)
- Highest accessibility score (10/10)
- Zero decorative overhead — content-first
- Best for design-forward, editorial, photography products

### 5. Highest Conversion Theme → **Cosmic Purple** (tied with Rose Gold)
- Both score 9/10 on conversion potential
- Cosmic Purple: urgency + premium = high buy intent
- Rose Gold: warmth + elegance = high trust purchases
- Recommendation: A/B test both for your specific audience
